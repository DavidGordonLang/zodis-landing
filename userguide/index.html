Beta 3 user guide

Getting the most from Žodis

Žodis combines a guided Lithuanian course with practical translation, pronunciation help, real-life scenarios and a personal phrase Library. This guide explains what each part does and how they fit together.

Personalised learning

Your profile

Žodis asks for a few details because Lithuanian changes depending on the person speaking and the situation. Your profile can include your preferred first name, speaker gender, date of birth, where you are from and where you live.

Those details let lessons use examples that are actually about you. For example, age lines can use your real age and country exercises can use your real origin instead of forcing you to memorise a fictional learner’s answer.

You can edit your profile later in Settings.

Quick practical Lithuanian

Home: translate, listen and save

Home is the quickest way to work out something you want to say outside the structured course.

  • Type what you want to say, or use the microphone where available.
  • Set the context so Žodis knows who is speaking, who you are speaking to and whether the tone should be friendly or polite.
  • Choose English-style phonetics or IPA if you want pronunciation support.
  • Play the Lithuanian aloud, then save useful results to your Library.
  • You can also add useful phrases to a personal Scenario collection for later.
Why context matters: Lithuanian can change with gender, number, relationship and formality. The controls are there so the app does not pretend one generic translation fits every conversation.

The main learning path

The guided course

Beta 3 includes a structured course across five sections. It starts with greetings and introductions, then develops through practical conversation, numbers and time, food and drink, directions, places and everyday travel language.

Training shows the next lesson to continue, while Browse course lets you revisit completed material or see where you are in the wider course.

Lessons

Short chunks introduce and practise language before asking you to use it.

Module checkpoints

Bring several lessons together so you retrieve rather than simply repeat.

Section checkpoints

Test a broader mix of the section before you move on.

Review

Completed lessons remain available so you can revisit them without losing completion.

How lessons work

Lesson activities

Žodis deliberately mixes several types of practice. You should not spend the whole course doing multiple choice.

  • Learn: a new word, phrase or useful transformation is introduced before it is tested.
  • Meaning choices: choose what a Lithuanian phrase means, often from deliberately close English options.
  • Build the phrase: assemble a Lithuanian phrase from tokens. You can submit an incomplete or overfilled attempt and be told what is wrong.
  • Match the pairs: connect Lithuanian with its meaning. Larger reviews are grouped into manageable pages.
  • Say it out loud: speak the phrase yourself and get simple learner-facing feedback.
  • Scenario practice: use the language in a short authored conversation rather than an isolated exercise.

When Žodis accepts a response that is understandable but not the best wording, it can let you continue while explaining the stronger option instead of treating every variation as simply wrong.

Real-life practice

Scenario conversations inside lessons

Lesson scenarios are authored conversations for situations such as cafés, introductions, directions, shopping, asking for help and travel. These are different from the personal Scenario collections described later in this guide.

Each lesson scenario starts with a short introduction so you know where you are, who you are speaking to and what you are trying to achieve.

  • Some turns ask you to produce Lithuanian yourself.
  • Some turns test comprehension by asking you to choose the correct English meaning of spoken Lithuanian.
  • When English is used only to test understanding, the conversation still continues with an authored Lithuanian learner line.
  • If a natural alternative works, Žodis can accept it and suggest a better or more precise phrasing.
  • Help is staged: a clue or repeated Lithuanian can come before a full English reveal.

Pronunciation

Audio, word taps and speaking

Audio is meant to be used constantly, not saved for a special pronunciation lesson.

  • Tap phrase audio for normal playback.
  • Hold or use the slow control where available when you need more time to hear the sounds.
  • Tap individual Lithuanian words in supported views to hear them on their own.
  • Use English-style phonetics if IPA is unfamiliar, or switch to IPA if you prefer a more exact notation.
  • In speaking checks, listen first, then say the phrase yourself.

The speech checker is designed for practical learner feedback. It does not show raw recognition diagnostics during normal use.

Keep moving

Progress, Resume and Review

Course progress is tied to your signed-in account and the current curriculum version.

  • If you leave part-way through a lesson, Resume returns you to a safe point rather than pretending the lesson never started.
  • Wrong answers remain part of the lesson result after closing and reopening.
  • Completed lessons stay completed after reload.
  • Review lets you run through a completed lesson again without turning it back into an unfinished lesson.
  • XP, level and streak information make overall activity visible on the Training screen.
Beta note: major curriculum changes can occasionally create a new course version. When that happens, old course progress may be treated as belonging to the earlier version. Your account and personal Library are separate from that course-version identity.

Keep useful language

Your personal Library

The Library holds the words and phrases you choose to keep. It is deliberately separate from “finishing the course”: you decide what deserves a permanent place in your own phrase collection.

  • Save useful translations from Home.
  • At course milestones, choose which reviewed vocabulary you want to keep rather than automatically saving everything.
  • Search and sort saved entries.
  • Play phrase and word audio.
  • Edit notes, context and other details later.
  • Use RAG status as a simple personal confidence marker: red, amber or green.

Daily Recall can optionally surface one saved phrase when you open the app, giving you a small piece of spaced review without demanding a full training session.

Your own collections

Personal Scenarios

The Scenarios tab also lets you build your own phrase collections around situations you personally need: a café visit, meeting family, an appointment, a trip or anything else.

You can add phrases from Home or the Library, reorder them and review them together. These personal Scenario collections are separate from the authored lesson conversations used inside the guided course.

Control your setup

Settings, Daily Recall and sync

Settings is where you can change the parts of Žodis that are specific to you.

  • Edit your profile.
  • Choose voice and phonetic preferences.
  • Turn Daily Recall on or off.
  • Reopen the in-app User Guide.
  • Manage Library and personal Scenario cloud sync.

Library / Scenario sync

Personal Library and Scenario syncing is intentionally controlled from Settings. Upload, Download and Merge have different effects, so read the confirmation text before choosing one. Merge is normally the safest option when both the device and cloud may contain useful changes.

Course progress uses its own account-based progress system and should not be confused with the Library sync controls.

While Žodis is in beta

What is finished, and what is still coming

Beta 3’s guided course, scenarios, Library, translation, audio and progress systems are live. Some additional Training areas are intentionally marked Coming soon rather than being unfinished parts of the current course.

  • Practice modes: additional standalone recognition/production/reinforcement tools are planned.
  • Exam prep: exam-style practice is planned for a later Beta 3.x update.

During the beta, the most useful feedback is specific: which screen you were on, what you expected to happen, what actually happened, and whether it blocked you or was simply confusing.

Access problem? First check that you are signing in with the exact email address approved for the beta. If the app still blocks you, reply to your beta welcome email or contact David through the channel that invited you.
